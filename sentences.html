Sentences

必须背熟的短句

短句是单词和语法的结合。你每天读 10 句，作文、阅读、语感都会变稳。

零基础短句

I am a student. 我是一名学生。I like English. 我喜欢英语。I study every day. 我每天学习。She is my friend. 她是我的朋友。He works hard. 他努力工作。We live in China. 我们住在中国。This book is useful. 这本书有用。English is important. 英语很重要。I don't know. 我不知道。Can you help me? 你能帮我吗？What does it mean? 它是什么意思？I need more practice. 我需要更多练习。

阅读常见短句

More and more people use the Internet. 越来越多人使用互联网。Education plays an important role in life. 教育在生活中起重要作用。The problem is becoming serious. 这个问题正变得严重。There are several reasons for this. 这有几个原因。It is necessary to take action. 采取行动是必要的。Technology has changed our lives. 科技已经改变了我们的生活。People have different opinions. 人们有不同观点。The result depends on your effort. 结果取决于你的努力。This method is effective. 这个方法有效。We should protect the environment. 我们应该保护环境。

作文万能短句

In my opinion, English is useful. 在我看来，英语有用。First, we should make a plan. 首先，我们应该制定计划。Second, practice is very important. 第二，练习非常重要。Finally, we should never give up. 最后，我们不应该放弃。As a result, we can make progress. 因此，我们能取得进步。It is a good way to learn English. 这是学英语的好方法。We should make full use of time. 我们应该充分利用时间。Learning English takes time. 学英语需要时间。Nothing is impossible if we work hard. 如果努力，没有什么不可能。I believe I can pass the exam. 我相信我能通过考试。

长句入门

Although English is difficult, I will keep learning it. 虽然英语很难，但我会坚持学。If we read more, we will learn more words. 如果我们读得更多，就会学到更多单词。The book that I bought yesterday is useful. 我昨天买的书很有用。I know that grammar is important. 我知道语法很重要。When I meet a new word, I write it down. 当我遇到新单词，我把它写下来。Because I am a beginner, I need a clear plan. 因为我是零基础，我需要清晰计划。

雅思托福口语开口句

I would say that education matters a lot. 我会说教育非常重要。From my perspective, this is a practical choice. 从我的角度看，这是一个实际的选择。It depends on the situation. 这取决于具体情况。There are several reasons behind this trend. 这个趋势背后有几个原因。I used to think so, but now I have changed my mind. 我过去这么想，但现在改变了想法。One example that comes to mind is online learning. 我想到的一个例子是在线学习。This is something I have experienced personally. 这是我亲身经历过的事情。It has both advantages and disadvantages. 它既有优点也有缺点。I find it useful because it saves time. 我觉得它有用，因为它节省时间。If I had more time, I would practice speaking every day. 如果我有更多时间，我会每天练口语。People may have different opinions about this issue. 人们对这个问题可能有不同看法。In my daily life, I often use English to search for information. 在日常生活中，我经常用英语搜索信息。Compared with the past, people now have more choices. 和过去相比，现在人们有更多选择。The most important point is that it improves efficiency. 最重要的一点是它提高效率。I am not an expert, but I think the idea is reasonable. 我不是专家，但我认为这个想法合理。It may sound simple, but it is not easy to do. 这听起来简单，但做起来不容易。This experience taught me to be patient. 这段经历教会我要有耐心。I prefer learning in a quiet place. 我更喜欢在安静的地方学习。It is a good way to relax and learn at the same time. 这是一种既放松又学习的好方法。I believe this habit will help me in the long run. 我相信这个习惯从长远看会帮助我。

写作论证句

There is no doubt that technology has changed our lives. 毫无疑问，科技改变了我们的生活。It is widely believed that education can change a person's future. 人们普遍认为教育能改变一个人的未来。The main reason is that people need more opportunities. 主要原因是人们需要更多机会。This problem should not be ignored. 这个问题不应被忽视。Governments should take effective measures to solve it. 政府应该采取有效措施解决它。Individuals also have a responsibility to make a difference. 个人也有责任产生影响。Too much pressure may have a negative effect on health. 过多压力可能对健康有负面影响。A balanced lifestyle is important for both study and work. 平衡的生活方式对学习和工作都重要。In the long term, this policy may bring more benefits. 从长远看，这项政策可能带来更多好处。We should pay more attention to environmental protection. 我们应该更关注环境保护。This view is understandable, but it is not complete. 这个观点可以理解，但并不完整。Only in this way can we solve the problem effectively. 只有这样我们才能有效解决问题。It is necessary to strike a balance between freedom and responsibility. 有必要在自由和责任之间取得平衡。The development of society depends on both innovation and cooperation. 社会发展依赖创新和合作。What matters most is not speed but quality. 最重要的不是速度，而是质量。

图表和数据句

The number increased sharply from 2010 to 2020. 这个数字从 2010 到 2020 急剧上升。The percentage dropped slightly in the following year. 这个比例在下一年轻微下降。There was a steady rise in the number of students. 学生数量稳定上升。The figure remained stable during this period. 这个数字在这一时期保持稳定。The data shows a clear difference between the two groups. 数据显示两组之间有明显差异。Compared with men, women spent more time reading. 与男性相比，女性花更多时间阅读。The largest proportion was found in the education sector. 最大比例出现在教育领域。The trend suggests that online learning is becoming more popular. 这个趋势表明在线学习越来越受欢迎。By contrast, the figure for public transport was much lower. 相比之下，公共交通的数据低得多。Overall, the chart shows a positive trend. 总体来看，图表显示出积极趋势。

考研阅读长句骨架

What matters is not what we know, but how we use it. 重要的不是我们知道什么，而是如何使用它。The more choices people have, the harder it may be to make a decision. 人们选择越多，做决定可能越难。Although the policy seems strict, it may protect public interests. 尽管这项政策看起来严格，但它可能保护公共利益。Those who keep learning are more likely to adapt to change. 那些持续学习的人更可能适应变化。The book that I borrowed from the library explains this idea clearly. 我从图书馆借的那本书清楚解释了这个想法。People who live in big cities often face more pressure. 住在大城市的人通常面临更多压力。When resources are limited, efficiency becomes especially important. 当资源有限时，效率变得尤其重要。If education is improved, social mobility may also increase. 如果教育得到改善，社会流动性也可能提高。Because the evidence is limited, the conclusion should be treated carefully. 因为证据有限，这个结论应谨慎对待。Whether this method works depends on how it is used. 这种方法是否有效取决于如何使用。

听力场景短句

Could you repeat that, please? 你能重复一下吗？I'd like to make an appointment. 我想预约。How much is the ticket? 票多少钱？Where is the nearest subway station? 最近的地铁站在哪里？I need to check in first. 我需要先办理入住。The meeting has been put off. 会议被推迟了。Let me check my schedule. 我看一下我的日程。I'm calling to ask about the course. 我打电话来询问课程。Would you mind speaking more slowly? 你介意说慢一点吗？That sounds like a good idea. 那听起来是个好主意。I have trouble understanding the lecture. 我听懂讲座有困难。The library closes at six. 图书馆六点关门。You need to hand in the paper by Friday. 你需要周五前交论文。The price includes breakfast. 价格包含早餐。We are running out of time. 我们时间不够了。
